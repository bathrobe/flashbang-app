Review Scoring Model

What one answer changes, for the Stage 1 build. Three numbers are updated from the same answer and are otherwise independent. Exact amounts are placeholders to settle in the spec.

One answer, three updates

Stored values

NameBelongs toWhat it is
XPAtomRunning total of credit earned on this atom. Only increases.
LevelAtomWorked out from XP by fixed thresholds. Not stored separately. Never falls.
IntervalAtomDays to wait before the next review. Grows after a correct answer, shrinks after a wrong one.
Due dateAtomDate of last review plus the interval.
CloutLearnerA number staked with the confidence slider. Never below zero. Nothing to spend it on yet.
Learner levelLearnerWorked out from the sum of all atom XP.
Main atom, linked atomsQuestionOne main atom, plus zero or more linked atoms, each with a weight between 0 and 1.

A retry is a different question on the same main atom, shown later in the same session after a wrong first attempt. Linked atoms never have their interval changed in Stage 1.

Borrowed from Math Academy, and what was left out

  • Kept: one answer credits more than one topic, by a weight set when the content is written.
  • Changed: their weights sit on links between topics in a hierarchy and travel many hops. Ours sit on the question and travel one hop, because humanities ideas have no fixed "simpler topic" order.
  • Left out for now: linked credit moving due dates, penalties spreading to other topics, per-learner speed, and choosing the question that covers the most due atoms.
  • Same as theirs: XP and the schedule are separate numbers.
